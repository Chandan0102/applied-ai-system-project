# Medication Safety
Species: dog, cat, other
Condition: medication
Source: General medication safety guidance (educational summary)

## Rules
Only give medication prescribed by the pet's vet, at the dose and time the vet specified. Never give human medicine such as ibuprofen, acetaminophen or aspirin to pets. If a dose is missed, call the vet instead of doubling it. Any medication task in a schedule should be confirmed with the vet.
- Task: Give prescribed medication as directed by your vet | 5 min | daily | high | morning

## Storage
Keep all medicine out of reach of pets and children.
