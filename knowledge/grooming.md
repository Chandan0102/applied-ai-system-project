# Grooming
Species: dog, cat
Source: General grooming guidance (educational summary)

## Brushing
Short-haired pets need brushing about once a week, long-haired pets several times a week to prevent mats.
- Task: Brush coat | 10 min | weekly | low | afternoon

## Nails and teeth
Trim nails about every three to four weeks. Brush teeth several times a week if the pet tolerates it.
- Task: Brush teeth | 5 min | daily | low | evening
- Task: Trim nails | 10 min | weekly | low | afternoon
