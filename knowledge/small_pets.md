# Small Pets (rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters)
Species: rabbit, guinea pig, hamster, other
Source: General small pet care guidance (educational summary)

## Daily care and feeding
Rabbits and guinea pigs need unlimited hay, fresh vegetables daily, fresh water, and supervised time out of the cage each day. Spot-clean the enclosure daily.
- Task: Fresh hay, vegetables and water | 10 min | daily | high | morning
- Task: Supervised out-of-cage time | 30 min | daily | medium | evening
- Task: Spot-clean enclosure | 10 min | daily | medium | morning
