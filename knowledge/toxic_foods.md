# Toxic Foods
Species: dog, cat
Source: General pet food safety guidance (educational summary)

## Never feed
Chocolate, grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol (sugar-free sweetener), macadamia nuts, and alcohol are dangerous to dogs. Onions, garlic, lilies and chocolate are dangerous to cats. If a pet eats one of these, contact a vet or an animal poison hotline immediately.
