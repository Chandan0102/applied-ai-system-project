# Diabetic Pet Routines
Species: dog, cat
Condition: diabetic
Source: General guidance on diabetic pet management (educational summary)

## Consistency
Diabetic pets need meals and insulin at consistent times each day, usually about twelve hours apart. The exact insulin type, dose and timing must come from the pet's veterinarian. This system never decides a dose.
- Task: Scheduled meal (diabetic routine) | 10 min | daily | high | morning
- Task: Scheduled meal (diabetic routine) | 10 min | daily | high | evening
- Task: Insulin time as prescribed by your vet | 5 min | daily | high | morning

## Monitoring
Watch for increased thirst, frequent urination, lethargy or changes in appetite, and report them to the vet. Keep exercise steady, not sudden and intense.
- Task: Check water bowl and note thirst | 5 min | daily | medium | evening
