# Cat Care Guidelines
Species: cat
Source: General feline care guidance (educational summary)

## Feeding
Adult cats do best on two or more small measured meals a day, with fresh water always available. Follow the portion size on the food label or from your vet.
- Task: Measured breakfast | 5 min | daily | high | morning
- Task: Measured dinner | 5 min | daily | high | evening

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and fully change the litter weekly.
- Task: Scoop litter box | 5 min | daily | medium | morning

## Play
Cats benefit from 10 to 15 minutes of interactive play once or twice a day, such as wand toys.
- Task: Interactive play | 15 min | daily | medium | evening
