# Senior Pet Care
Species: dog, cat
Source: General senior pet care guidance (educational summary)

## Health checks
Senior pets (dogs about 8 and older, cats about 10 and older) should see a vet every six months. Watch weight, mobility, appetite, and dental health.
- Task: Quick health check (weight, mobility, appetite) | 5 min | weekly | medium | morning

## Comfort
Provide soft bedding, easy access to food and water, and non-slip floors. Keep routines predictable.
- Task: Check bedding and water access | 5 min | daily | low | morning
