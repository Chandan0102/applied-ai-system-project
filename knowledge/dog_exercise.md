# Dog Exercise Guidelines
Species: dog
Source: General veterinary exercise guidance (educational summary)

## Adult dogs
Most healthy adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of exercise per day, split into at least two outings. Active breeds may need more.
- Task: Morning walk | 30 min | daily | high | morning
- Task: Evening walk | 20 min | daily | medium | evening

## Senior dogs
Senior dogs (about 8 years and older) benefit from shorter, gentler, more frequent walks. Avoid hard running and jumping, and watch for stiffness or heavy panting.
- Task: Gentle short walk | 15 min | daily | high | morning
- Task: Gentle short walk | 15 min | daily | medium | evening

## Puppies
Puppies need several short play and training sessions rather than long walks, because their joints are still developing.
- Task: Short play and training session | 10 min | daily | high | morning
