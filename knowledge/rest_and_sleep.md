# Rest and Sleep
Species: dog, cat, rabbit, guinea pig, hamster, other
Condition: nap
Source: General pet rest guidance (educational summary)

## Naps and quiet time
Adult dogs and cats sleep many hours a day, and a predictable quiet period in the afternoon helps most pets settle. Provide a comfortable, quiet, draft-free spot. Needs vary with age, so puppies, kittens and seniors may nap more often. Mention changes in sleep patterns to your vet.
